About the Academy

A single roof for academic strength and digital skill.

Founded and led by R. Durka Ramesh, Sri Durka Academy is registered with the Tamilnadu Private Tuition Centre Association (TTCA) and operates as an ISO 9001:2015 certified institution in Krishnankovil. We believe strong fundamentals and modern technology skills should be taught under one roof — so every student leaves ready for the classroom and the career ahead.
