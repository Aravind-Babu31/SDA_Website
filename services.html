Academic Streams

Tuition, Abacus & Languages under one roof.

Beyond our technical coding streams, we offer three core academic services focused on child logic, language skills, and board syllabus readiness.

Service 01

School Tuitions (Online & Offline)

We provide focused, subject-oriented academic tutoring from 1st Std to 12th Std. Special board exam preparation batches are scheduled for 9th, 10th, 11th, and 12th Standard students covering Matriculation, CBSE, and Tamil Nadu State Board syllabi.

All Subjects Covered

Thorough coaching in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Social Sciences, and Languages.

Flexible Delivery Modes

Attend interactive live classes online or join micro-group face-to-face sessions at our Krishnankovil branch.

Mock Exams & Assessment

Weekly and monthly tests based on official board blueprints to track performance and remove exam fear.

Service 02

Advanced Abacus Training

A systematic mental arithmetic program mapped for children aged 4 to 16. Using visualization techniques and standard bead manipulation rules, we train young minds to calculate faster than calculators while boosting memory power.

Enhanced Math Calculation Speed

Enables quick mental calculation of multi-digit additions, subtractions, multiplications, and divisions.

Sharpens Attention & Concentration

Improves focus levels, listening capabilities, and overall classroom attention spans.

Photographic Brain Recall

Exercises the right brain hemisphere, building visual-spatial recall and conceptual memory.

Service 03

Spoken Classes (Hindi & English)

A practical, dialogue-first approach to spoken languages. Our curriculum focuses on breaking stage fright, expanding active vocabulary, structuring grammatical syntax, and mastering conversational flows.

Spoken English

Focuses on accent reduction, proper sentence conjugation, group discussions, and workplace vocabulary.

Spoken Hindi

Covers everyday conversational grammar, vocabulary blocks, translation, and communication styles.

Public Speaking Activities

Engage in mock presentations, dialogues, and telephone roleplays to eliminate speaking fear.
