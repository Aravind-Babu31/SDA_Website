What We Offer

Tuition, Abacus & Spoken Language — under one roof.

Beyond computer courses, Sri Durka Academy runs three core academic services designed around a child's growth: school tuition, abacus training, and spoken language classes.

Service 01

Tuition — Online & Offline

Comprehensive subject tuition for students from 1st Std to 12th Std, with dedicated focus batches for 9th, 10th, 11th and 12th Std, across State Board, Matriculation and CBSE syllabi. Choose in-person classes at our Krishnankovil campus or join online from home.

  • 1st Std to 12th Std, all subjects
  • Extra focus for 9th, 10th, 11th & 12th Std board years
  • State Board, Matriculation and CBSE curricula covered
  • Flexible online or offline attendance
Enquire About Tuition

Service 02

Advanced Abacus Training

A structured abacus programme for children aged 4 to 16, designed to build mental arithmetic ability far beyond classroom pace, using consistent practice and progressive levels.

  • Faster calculation skills
  • Strong memory power
  • Boosts overall confidence
  • Suitable for ages 4 through 16
Enquire About Abacus

Service 03

Spoken Class — Hindi & English

Practical, conversation-first spoken language classes in Hindi and English, focused on everyday communication, grammar basics and building the confidence to speak freely.

  • Spoken English classes
  • Spoken Hindi classes
  • Grammar fundamentals reinforced through conversation
