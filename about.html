About Sri Durka Academy

Built on discipline, guided by care.

A TTCA registered, ISO 9001:2015 certified academy in Krishnankovil — bringing academic tuition and computer education together under one commitment to every learner's growth.

Officially Registered

Recognised by the Tamilnadu Private Tuition Centre Association

Sri Durka Academy operates as a registered member of TTCA (Tamilnadu Private Tuition Centre Association), a state-level body for private tuition and coaching centres, under its Dharmapuri state head office. Alongside this, the academy holds ISO 9001:2015 certification as a quality-managed institution.

Why We Exist

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To give every student — from 1st Std to 12th Std — personal attention and a strong academic foundation, while equipping the next generation with real, employable computer skills, all within a single, disciplined learning environment.

Our Vision

To be Krishnankovil's most trusted academy for holistic learning — where school subjects, life skills like spoken language and abacus, and industry-relevant technology courses are taught with equal seriousness and care.
